Support Worker wanted: Armadale to Willetton.

We're looking for a reliable, caring and experienced Disability Support Worker who lives in or near Armadale to provide school transport support for a young NDIS participant, every school day until the end of Term 4.

Apply by email WhatsApp 0459 676 429
The role at a glance

Two school runs a day, the same friendly face.

  • LocationArmadale to Willetton, WA
  • DaysMonday to Friday (school days)
  • DurationAbout 8 weeks, to the end of Term 4
  • Morning run7:30 am pick-up in Armadale, school drop-off in Willetton by 8:30 am
  • Afternoon runSchool pick-up around 2:30 pm, return to Armadale
  • Start dateAs soon as possible

About the role

  • Provide safe, reliable school transport for a secondary-school student with autism who communicates non-verbally.
  • Support communication using sign language, gestures and communication devices.
  • Keep each trip calm, supportive and consistent.
  • Be available for both the morning and afternoon school runs.

Consistency matters. We're looking for one support worker to do every run, so the participant sees the same familiar face each day.

What you'll need

  • Experience supporting children or young people with autism
  • Understanding of non-verbal communication needs
  • Current NDIS Worker Screening Check
  • Working With Children Check
  • Valid driver's licence and a reliable, insured vehicle
  • Punctual, patient, compassionate and dependable
  • Ideally based in Armadale or nearby suburbs
