Our services

Core NDIS supports, delivered with care.

Everyday, practical help that keeps you safe, independent and connected — in your home and out in your community.

  • Assistance with daily life

    Personal routines, hygiene, meal prep and the day-to-day support that keeps you living independently at home.

  • Community participation

    Getting out, staying active and building confidence — social activities, hobbies, appointments and connection.

  • Domestic assistance

    Cleaning, laundry and general upkeep, so your home stays safe, comfortable and easy to manage.

  • Personal care

    Respectful, dignified help with bathing, dressing, grooming and mobility — always at your pace.

  • Transport & access

    Safe, reliable travel to appointments, activities and errands — so nothing gets in the way of your day.

About StemsCare

A Western Australian team that treats care as a partnership.

StemsCare Services is a Perth-based NDIS provider founded by two women with real, personal experience of caring for family members with disability. We know how overwhelming the system can feel — so we walk alongside you and your family, helping you understand your options and use your plan with confidence.

Our support is built on warmth, respect and consistency: familiar faces, clear communication, and care shaped around your goals rather than ours.

A values-driven team, guided by Christian principles of compassion and service — and welcoming to people of every faith, culture and background.
